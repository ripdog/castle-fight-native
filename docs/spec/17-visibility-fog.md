# Visibility and fog of war

Visibility is authoritative, integer, and shared by team. The selected map version MUST supply resolved day/night sight for every unit, builder appearance, construction shell, and building. Attack range and acquisition range MUST NOT substitute for sight. The day/night clock advances in simulation ticks using the selected version's retained Warcraft defaults and map overrides.

Living entities reveal terrain within their current sight. Ground sight stops behind authored sight blockers and higher cliff levels; flying sight bypasses these obstacles. Corpses, projectiles, and delayed callback entities do not contribute ordinary sight. Navigation obstacles and smooth terrain height alone MUST NOT become vision blockers. Explicit script fog modifiers bypass occlusion.

The binary grid distinguishes currently visible cells and permanently explored cells. The selected map's fog-mask policy and permanent team/shared regions come from its retained script projection. An unexplored map uses black shroud; an explored cell outside current vision uses fog. Smooth edges and reveal/conceal animation are presentation only and MUST NOT affect targeting, checksums, or spell timing.

Ordinary attack acquisition and retention require team visibility. Invisible enemies additionally require active detection or a revealing status. Faerie Fire's existing timed revealing status follows its target, exposes it to the marking team, and shares that target's current day/night sight. Purification's extracted Far Sight proxy grants stationary area vision and detection. Committed ordinary attacks originating outside the victim team's sight grant a temporary source-centered vision flash using the selected version's Warcraft miscellaneous defaults. Ordinary script fog grants reveal terrain without detecting invisible enemies. Script target enumeration and global effects retain their source-defined eligibility; a native target order can cross fog only when its script explicitly grants target vision.

Timed area grants carry team, center, radius, detection semantics, and an exclusive simulation-tick expiry. Exploration, grants, and last-observed enemy structure identities/footprints and frozen observed construction phases MUST survive checksum, save, replay, and rejoin. Current visibility is derived from these inputs and living entities and is rebuilt on restore and at deterministic phase boundaries. The immutable grid layout, clock, blocker geometry, and permanent grants participate in configuration compatibility.

An observer client MUST hide unseen enemy units, builders, corpses, and missiles and suppress concealed effects and target references. Previously observed enemy structures retain a silhouette until their old location is observed again; destruction, upgrades, health, production, mana, and status changes in fog MUST NOT update that silhouette. Remembered silhouettes cannot be selected or expose live inspection data. Omniscient diagnostic/profiling views MAY bypass observer filtering.

The client samples the team grid in world coordinates, uses spatial filtering for soft edges, and applies a frame-rate independent, reversible fade. Save loads, observer changes, and timeline rewinds reset cosmetic history. Rendering and wall-clock time MUST NOT feed back into the simulation.
