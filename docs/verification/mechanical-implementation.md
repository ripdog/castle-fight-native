# Mechanical implementation

Retained Castle Fight 9.27/r1 extraction tree: `8ea806dca331ff254995e94e6f0baf225a14bf10`. Builder `X06P` ("Mech Coalition"), registration `EK` and the generated `catalog-supplement` build-card coordinates define implementation order. Complete each root and its entire upgrade chain before moving right, then the next row. Only completed entities become buildable. The order is Aircraft Factory → Bomber Factory; Tank Factory → Light Tank line (Mine Layer Factory, Assault Tank Factory, Heavy Tank Factory → Rocket Tank Factory); Demolition Lab → Rocket Lab; Gun Smith → Arsenal; Golem Factory → Precious Golem Factory; Lumber Mill → Protection Center; Blue Shield Generator → Red Shield Generator; Spare Part Heap; Energy Tower; Power Plant; the shared Treasure Box (already promoted).

## Aircraft Factory / Roterothopter

- Identity/source: runtime r187; `h05J` (`hbla`) produces `h06Q` (`hgyr`) in the top-left slot and upgrades to the Bomber Factory. Protected life, armor, weapon damage/dice/cooldown/range and the retained flying movement supersede static sentinels. Corpse, collision, classification (`Mechanical`, sapper, ward), production, construction/repair/economy and upgrade data consume the retained catalogs. The building's only ability `A07E` is the shared Channel marker already bound for the Sniper Nest.
- Weapons: two ordinary enabled instant Pierce weapons, one that targets air only and one that targets structures only (retained secondary-weapon primitive; neither can attack ground units). The importer's `xO` summary reports range 999 (the sum of both weapon ranges minus one); the retained weapon rows carry the real per-weapon range, which is what the engine uses.
- Complete inventory/control flow: the sole ability is `A09I` (`ACbh`, "Stun Rounds"), registered object data with no script consumer and no hidden proxy. It is the native Bash passive with a map-overridden target mask of air only: the chance, bonus damage, normal and hero stun durations all come from the retained row, and the multiplier, miss and never-miss fields are zero. Because the mask names a movement class, it proc-rolls only on air attacks (structures and ground units never roll).
- State: none beyond ordinary proc rolls. Mechanical becomes selectable with this completed root; later roots remain unavailable until their own mechanics are verified.

## Bomber Factory / Gyrocopter

- Identity/source: runtime r188; `h09L` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Aircraft Factory and produces `h06R` (`hgyr`). Protected life, armor and weapon damage/dice/cooldown/range supersede static sentinels; the flying body's movement, collision, corpse and classification (`Mechanical`, sapper, ward) data are the same retained classes as the Roterothopter's. The building's `A07E` is the shared Channel marker.
- Weapons: an instant Pierce weapon that targets air only, and a **secondary artillery Siege weapon that targets structures only** (range, cooldown and damage from the protected overlay/static rows). The artillery weapon is a splash projectile: the retained object fields give it its *own* missile speed and its own full/half/quarter area bands, damage factors and splash target mask, none of which are the primary weapon's (the primary has no splash and the resolved unit table only carries attack-1 splash columns). They are now projected into the catalog supplement (`secondary_artillery_weapons`, schema 11) from `resolved/object-fields.tsv` rather than being read from the primary weapon's columns.
- Engine note: secondary weapons previously accepted only instant and missile deliveries. A secondary artillery weapon now becomes a ballistic delivery whose speed and outer radius come from that projection; its bands ride on the existing unit-level splash passive, which only ballistic/homing projectiles read (an instant primary ignores it). The loader refuses a body that would carry splash bands on both weapons, because the passive is not weapon-scoped.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A09J` (`ACbh`) is the same air-only Bash passive as the Roterothopter's, with its own retained chance, bonus damage and durations; no script consumer or hidden proxy. A ballistic structure attack never rolls it (its mask names air).
- Discrepancy: the tooltip's "36 dps" for the structure weapon is the protected damage over the 2.5 s cooldown before splash falloff; runtime data wins.

## Mine Layer Factory / Mine Layer

- Identity/source: runtime r190; `h09J` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Tank Factory and produces `h06U` (`hmtt`). Protected life, armor (7), movement, the Normal weapon (ground and structures; the second Pierce air weapon is disabled and unavailable) and the retained mana pool (50, +2.3/s) come from the catalogs. Corpse, collision, classification, production, construction/repair/economy and upgrade data consume the retained catalogs.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A03U` is the shared hidden Channel marker, `A0A0` the Light Tank's Light Plating (same ability object, same row), and `A095` (`ACpa`, "Minefield") the only scripted ability. Its native Parasite parent is autocast, costs the protected 25 mana, has no cooldown and a map-wide range, and carries a 0.01 s buff with a single Fel Boar death-summon body that is never a practical effect; the script child (`mineLayerSpell`, registered by `FK`) is the real consequence. `onUnitTrained` additionally gives a freshly trained Mine Layer a uniformly random mana in the retained range (`train-event-random-initial-mana`), replacing the static starting mana of 12.
- Scripted spell trace: trigger eligibility — the native autocast Parasite; the engine fires it at a visible hostile ground combat sapper (the shared parent rule). Approach — none beyond the map-wide range. Effect origin — the **caster**, not the Parasite target: a uniformly random angle and a uniformly random distance in the retained range around the caster's position at cast time. Effect — a dummy carrier flies to that point at the retained speed; the script waits distance ÷ speed seconds (rounded up to whole ticks), then creates the Landmine (`n025`: locust, `Amnx`, `Amin`) there for the caster's owner. Effect eligibility is independent: the mine arms after the retained `Amin` delay, then detonates when any enemy, non-invulnerable ground unit comes within the retained radius; its native death explosion lands after the retained post-death delay, damaging enemy ground units and structures in two rings (full and half damage) as spell damage through the shared Amnx rules. The mine has no lifetime and persists after its planter dies; a dying planter keeps explosion credit through the retained damage-source rules.
- Resources: the parent's protected mana only; the planted mine and carrier cost nothing, and a Parasite whose target cannot be hit still plants (the child ignores the target).
- Engine note: `LandMine` is a new parasite-script child and an authoritative native action (canonical tag 43); the explosion reuses the shared death-explosion action. Deterministic placement has no trigonometry: a direction uniform in angle comes from rejection sampling inside a circle, then is scaled to the sampled distance. A trained caster's random mana is drawn at spawn from the match seed and unit id (production only), as 1/10000-mana fixed point. Snapshot schema 109 and checksum schema 106 cover the new state.
- Known discrepancies: the importer's native Parasite mask also names structures, mechanical and invulnerable targets, so the original autocast could fire at an enemy building even before an enemy unit is visible; the engine's parent rule fires only at a visible hostile ground combat unit. The retained `Amin` `Min1`/`Min2` fields are read like the Echo remnant's (`Min1` is the activation delay); no Warcraft runtime oracle exists for the other field. The Parasite parent's Fel Boar death-summon is ignored on the Mine Layer's own parent as for the other script parents; the inert building Parasite parents carry an armed vanilla base body, which the engine now ends without a spawn instead of failing.

## Demolition Lab / Lunatic Goblin

- Identity/source: runtime r194; `h05M` (`hbla`) produces `n01V` (`ngsp`) in the third slot of the top row and upgrades to the Rocket Lab. Protected life (175), armor type, movement and the retained mana pool (100, unused by any ability) come from the catalogs. The ordinary Chaos melee weapon has a literal dash for its base damage (the native integer zero), so its damage is the dice alone; the importer's average column is blank for that reason and the loader now derives it from the dice instead of rejecting the body. The unit leaves no corpse and is classified as a sapper.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` is the shared hidden Channel marker; `A099` (`Asds`, "Dreams of Grandeur") is the object's default-active (`udaa`) native Self Destruct; `A0A3` (`Amnx`, "AOE damage upon death") is the native post-death area damage. No script consumer, proxy or hidden marker exists on this body.
- Spell trace (`A099`): trigger eligibility — the native autocast fires for the nearest visible hostile ground unit or structure within the unit's acquisition range (the mask is `enemies,ground,structure,tree,ward`, no air). Approach/cast geometry — the body walks (on its own ground topology) to body contact plus the retained 10-unit cast range, instead of stopping at its weapon range. Effect origin — the caster. Effect eligibility is independent of the trigger: living hostile ground units and structures in two rings (retained full and partial radii and damage), the structure damage scaled by the retained building factor measured at footprint distance, as physical ability damage that uses armor points without attack-class multipliers (the shared Asds rule). Resources — free, no cooldown. Timing — the explosion releases at the unit's cast point after the cast starts; the caster is killed by the cast (a suicide, not a damage kill). The retained `Explodes on Death` flag is 0 here and is corpse appearance only.
- Spell trace (`A0A3`): once the Lunatic dies — whether by its own Kaboom or enemy damage — the native Amnx area damage follows after the retained 0.3 s post-death delay at the death position, as spell damage with the universal spell armor table to hostile ground units and structures. The tooltip's 425 is the sum of the two full-ring amounts.
- Engine change: object-native Asds is now a passive `ContactSelfDestruct` shared with the Greater Elemental of Wind's script-armed Kaboom (one contact/detonation implementation); the profile gained the native cast range (zero for the script-granted variant) and the unit-ability tuning row replaces the old ability-effect-only restriction. Movement for such a bearer now follows its own ground or air topology instead of always using the air topology. New authoritative field: the in-progress detonation tick (status, canonical and snapshot state).
- Known tooltip/object discrepancy: the tooltip's "reduced by 16% for buildings" does not match the retained building damage factor (0.75 on the Asds ring); runtime data wins.

## Lumber Mill / Goblin Shredder

- Identity/source: runtime r200; `h05U` (`hbla`) is produced from the second slot of the second row, produces `n01T` (`ngir`) and upgrades to the Protection Center. Protected life, armor (Medium), movement and the Pierce melee weapon (ground and structures; the second weapon is a disabled tree-only slot, unavailable) come from the catalogs. Corpse, collision and classification data are the retained classes.
- Complete inventory/control flow: four object abilities. `A094` (`ACce`, "Spinning Blades") is the native Cleaving Attack with the map's distributed-damage factor of 100% over the base area, ground enemies only; the shared cleave primitive applies. The other three form Engine Overheat and have no standalone effect: `A00J` (`Aspb`) is the display spell book whose level mirrors the overheat level, `A09C` (`AIs2`) the attack-speed item ability whose per-level increase is the overheat attack-speed bonus, and `A09B` (`Amnx`) the death explosion whose per-level amounts are the overheat death damage. Their rows are consumed by the existing building-spell overheat state (the shared shield/overheat engine already used by every hostile building spell): the Shredder has a 60% chance to block each negative building ability, **a failed roll does not fall through to the legendary anti-hex check**, and every successful block raises the overheat level by one up to the retained maximum (levels 1–5), scaling the attack-speed bonus and the death-explosion damage together. Bindings are marker-only because the runtime is the already-audited script-native overheat shield, not these rows' own Warcraft behavior.
- Scripted spell trace (`hasShield`, all negative building spells): trigger — any hostile building ability about to affect the unit; resource — none; effect — the ability's body is skipped when blocked; a block still raises the overheat level. The overheat death explosion is resolved by the shared overheat death phase.
- Known tooltip/object discrepancy: the tooltip's attack-speed range ("0-200%", +50% per hit) and explosion range (50-800, ×2 per hit) are the retained per-level tables consumed by the overheat state.
- State: the overheat level lives in the existing building-spell target state (already canonical and snapshotted).
