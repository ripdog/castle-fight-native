# Undead implementation

Retained Castle Fight 9.27/r1 extraction tree: `8ea806dca331ff254995e94e6f0baf225a14bf10`. Builder `X018` ("Undead Builder", `uabi` `Aeth,A06E,AInv,A005,A001,Ahrp,Avul`, `urac` undead) and the generated `catalog-supplement` build-card coordinates define implementation order. Complete each root and its entire upgrade chain before moving right, then the next row. Only completed entities become buildable. The order is Rotting Hay Cart → Tainted Farm → Defiled Granary; Temple of Bone → Temple of Necromancy → High Citadel of Necromancy; Crypt → Graveyard; Haunted Mine; Slaughter House; Dragon Cemetery; Death Pit; Skull Pile → Skull Shrine; Turret of Souls; City of Decay; the shared Treasure Box (already promoted). (`Aeth`, `AInv`, `Ahrp`, `Avul` and the build-menu abilities are the shared builder body already used by every promoted race.)

## Rotting Hay Cart / Zombie

- Identity/source: runtime r207; `h01R` (`hbla`) produces `n00B` (`nzom`) in the top-left slot and upgrades to the Tainted Farm. Protected life, armor, movement and the Chaos melee weapon (ground and structures; the second weapon is disabled and unavailable) come from the catalogs. Corpse data (`can-raise-decays`, with the retained decay timers) is carried by the corpse catalog; Raise Dead is a later Necromancer ability and consumes it unchanged.
- Complete inventory/control flow: the sole ability is `A01L` (`ANpi`, "Permanent Immolation"), registered object data with no script consumer and no hidden proxy. It is the shared native permanent Immolation primitive (`warcraft-permanent-immolation-v1`, also used by the Infernal): radius, damage per interval, interval and target mask come from the retained row. Unlike the Infernal's variant its mask is organic-only, so mechanical bodies are never burned (the recipe projects the `organic` qualifier).
- State: none beyond the shared Immolation pulse phase.
- Undead becomes selectable with this completed root; later roots remain unavailable until their own mechanics are verified.

## Tainted Farm / Suffocated Zombie

- Identity/source: runtime r208; `h04Z` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Rotting Hay Cart (and leads to the Defiled Granary) and produces `n01K` (`nzom`). Protected life, armor, movement and the Chaos melee weapon come from the catalogs; the second weapon is disabled and unavailable.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A05Q` (`ACvs`, "Envenomed Weapons") and `A05P` (`ANpi`, "Permanent Immolation"), both registered object data with no script consumer, proxy or hidden marker. `A05Q` is the shared native poison primitive (`warcraft-poison-v1`): per-second damage, movement factor (the retained `DataB` slow), attack-speed factor (zero), duration, the shorter hero duration, per-source stacking and the organic-only non-lethal pulses all come from the retained row. `A05P` is the same organic-only native Immolation as the Zombie's with its own retained radius and damage.
- State: ordinary poison and Immolation state only.

## Defiled Granary / Plague Bearer

- Identity/source: runtime r209; `h03M` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Tainted Farm (the end of the line) and produces `u007` (`uabo`). Protected life, armor, movement and the Chaos melee weapon come from the catalogs.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A05T` (`ANpi`, "Permanent Immolation") and `A05S` (`ACvs`, "Envenomed Weapons"; "Greater Plague" buff) are the same shared native primitives as the Zombie line, with their own retained radius, damage, slow and duration. `A05R` (`Aap1`, "Disease Cloud") is object-native Plague aura data with no script consumer or hidden proxy (registered ability coverage: `native-object-owned`).
- Disease Cloud: the retained row gives the area (350), the infection length (`DataA`, 120 s), the damage per second (`DataB`, 18) and the lifetime of the plague ward an infected body leaves where it dies (`DataC`, 5 s); the ward unit id `uplg` and its own aura row are stock Warcraft data not retained in the extraction. Trigger/effect eligibility — hostile (enemy-team), alive, non-invulnerable, non-spell-immune, non-Locust organic ground or air bodies within the area of the carrier (and, after a death, within the same area of the ward). Infection — a non-stacking native poison family (the buff is the stock `Bapl`): damage clock once per second from the first application, lethal, refreshed to the full retained length once a second while the body stays inside an area. Death — the ward appears at the dead body's position for the retained ward time, belongs to the carrier's team, credits the original carrier and infects with the same infection, so a chain of deaths spreads it. Resources — free, no cooldown.
- Engine change: `DiseaseCloud` is a new passive primitive over the shared native poison (the poison profile gained an optional plague-ward descriptor), `PlagueWard` a new authoritative action (canonical tag 44) and the passive hashes tag 46; snapshot schema 110, checksum schema 107.
- Known discrepancies: the ward area and the infection refresh cadence (once a second) are inferred from the retained area and the stock aura refresh behavior; no Warcraft runtime oracle exists for either. An infected body that leaves every area keeps the infection for the retained length (it does not end when the carrier dies).

## Temple of Bone / Necromancer

- Identity/source: runtime r210; `h01K` (`hbla`) produces `u005` (`unec`) in the second slot of the top row and upgrades to the Temple of Necromancy. Protected life, armor, movement, the mana pool (200, starting 150) and the weapon come from the catalogs. The raised body `u004` ("Ghoul", base `ugho`) is a plain promoted unit (no abilities) that is registered only as a Raise Dead body; it consumes its own retained weapon, armor and corpse data.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` is the shared hidden Channel marker; `A01Y` (`Arai`, "Raise Dead") is registered object data with no script consumer and is the unit's default-active ability (`udaa`), so the unit casts it on its own. No hidden proxy exists on this body.
- Spell trace (`A01Y`): trigger eligibility — a usable (decay start reached) corpse of any team within the retained area (900) of the caster whose death type allows raising (`udea` bit 0; mechanical and cannot-raise bodies are excluded). Approach/cast geometry — none; the order is instant and centered on the caster (the retained `Rng1` 600 is not used by a no-target order; the area is the real reach). Effect origin — each corpse's own position. Effect eligibility — the same corpse test, independent of the trigger: up to the retained count (`DataA`, 1) of the nearest qualifying corpses around the caster, ordered by distance then `SimId`, are consumed and the retained body (`DataC`) is created at each for the caster's owner and team, marked summoned. Resources — the protected mana cost (75) and the protected cooldown (45 s); a cast with no corpse costs nothing. Persistence — the retained duration is zero, so the raised bodies are permanent (a positive duration would attach a timed life).
- Engine change: `RaiseDead` is a new ability effect with a `RaisableCorpse` target policy; both are validated at resolution against the live corpse. The corpse catalog now retains the map's raise flag for production bodies and summon bodies (supplement schema field `can_raise`).
- Known discrepancies: the importer's second unit type (`DataD`, a placeholder) is ignored as it names no body; whether the original AI cast Raise Dead from a corpse beyond the 600 cast range cannot be checked without a Warcraft runtime oracle.

## Temple of Necromancy / Mighty Necromancer

- Identity/source: runtime r211; `h04B` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Temple of Bone (and leads to the High Citadel) and produces `u009` (`uktg`). Protected life, armor, movement, mana pool and the Magic missile weapon (ground, structures and air; `Rng` 400) come from the catalogs. The raised body `u008` ("Greater Ghoul") is registered only as a Raise Dead body, like the Necromancer's `u004`.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` the shared hidden Channel marker; `A05O` (`Asph`, "Sphere"), the shared native Sphere with its own retained row; `A05L` (`Arai`, "Raise Dead"), the Necromancer's native order with the retained larger raised body, its own protected cooldown (30 s) and the same default-active autocast; `A05N` (`AIlx`, "carrion swarm") is a native attack-proc orb that casts the child `A05M` on the weapon's target. `A05M` (`AUcs`, "Carrion Swarm") is the native expanding cone over the retained `Data`/`Area` fields, with no script consumer. No hidden proxy exists on this body.
- Spell trace (`A05N` → `A05M`): trigger eligibility — each landed weapon hit rolls the retained chance (25%; heroes, summons and units share it) against targets in the orb's mask (air, ground and buildings, not wards-only). Effect origin — the orb child is cast by the attacker toward the target, the cone starting at the caster. Effect eligibility — independent of the orb's mask: the child's mask is exactly air+ground units (the stock mask's organic qualifier removed by the map, so mechanical and non-organic bodies are damaged) and, like stock Carrion Swarm, never an ally; structures are not hit. Damage is the retained value with its (unreachable) maximum cap; the wave expands from the retained initial to final radius over the retained distance at the retained missile speed. Resources — the child costs no mana and has a zero protected cooldown (the previous cone children left a one-tick native cooldown).
- Engine note: the cone-wave projection now accepts the air+ground variant of stock Carrion Swarm and records the actual protected child cooldown.

## High Citadel of Necromancy / Lich King

- Identity/source: runtime r212; `h055` (`hbla`) upgrades from the Temple of Necromancy (the end of the line) and produces `u00D` (`unec`). Protected life, armor, movement, the mana pool (400, starting 250) and the Magic missile weapon (ground, structures, air; range 500) come from the catalogs. The raised body `u00E` ("Lich Ghoul") is registered only as a Raise Dead body.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV`, `A08X` and `A07H` are hidden Channel markers (the last is `TypeLegendary`); `A06V` is the shared legendary Sphere marker and `A070` the legendary Moon Glaive marker, which the devour branch reads as an immunity; `A0AH` is the shared native spell-resistance row (one recipe serves every legendary bearer); `A03Q` is the shared source-damage dispatcher marker, which gates `handleSourceDamageEffects` for this body; `A085` (`Arai`) is the Necromancer's Raise Dead with the retained count of two, its own raised body, protected mana cost (100) and cooldown (25 s); `A086` (`AUdd`, "Death And Decay") is registered object data that no order of the unit ever issues — the script grants it to a dummy.
- Scripted spell trace (`devourAttackProc`, dispatched by the damage listener for source type `u00D` while the bearer carries `A03Q`): trigger eligibility — each landed weapon hit (including a hit that kills its target) rolls one integer in 0..99 against two thresholds; the roll is partitioned: below 15 and an eligible target devours, otherwise below 25 (including an ineligible target that rolled below 15) releases Death and Decay. Devour eligibility — a living, non-mechanical unit not carrying the legendary marker `A070`; a building is never eligible. Devour effect — the bearer regains half of the target's current life (before the damage) up to its maximum, then the target takes the retained 10000 chaos/death attack damage, which ignores armor (invulnerable bodies take none). The cast origin of Death and Decay is the damaged target's position at hit time; effect eligibility is independent: hostile air or ground units (the map removed stock's structure token, so the retained building reduction never applies and structures are not damaged) inside the retained area (350), each losing the retained 8% of its maximum life once per second for the retained five seconds, as spell damage credited to the bearer. Resources — none. Persistence — the area is an authoritative action that outlives the target and the bearer.
- Engine change: `DeathMastery` is a new passive primitive resolved at the attack roll into a triggered attack effect (canonical tag 8, passive tag 47); `DeathAndDecay` a new authoritative action (canonical tag 45). The devour kills and heals through the ordinary health commit with source credit. Snapshot schema 111, checksum schema 108.
- Known discrepancies: the script's devour damage is itself an attack-flagged damage event that would re-enter the listener for the same bearer; the engine resolves one proc per landed attack (the nested roll would need a second chain of probabilities). First pulse timing (one second after the cast, five pulses), the use of spell damage (not universal) and the exclusion of spell-immune victims are inferred from the stock aura behavior; no Warcraft runtime oracle exists. The devour heal rounds down.
