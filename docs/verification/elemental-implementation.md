- Complete inventory: `A0DS`/`A0F5` remain the previously audited classification markers. `A0CQ` is a native `AOae` with negative movement/attack rates, hostile air recipients and zero linger. Mechanical recipients are allowed; allies, ground units, buildings and spell-immune enemies are excluded. Invisible recipients remain eligible for an area aura; an invisible or Hex-suppressed source does not contribute. `ward` permits the native ward category rather than restricting the aura exclusively to ward objects. Visual flights retain air/ground origin and destination height even when their source or target is gone, and use the release destination; this deterministic native presentation interpretation is explicitly separate from the weapon's hit delivery.
