- Complete inventory: `A0DS`/`A0F5` remain the previously audited classification markers. `A0CQ` is a native `AOae` with negative movement/attack rates, hostile air recipients and zero linger. Its one source buff family determines stacking: each stat selects its strongest eligible contributor independently, with immediate fallback/removal when membership changes. Mechanical recipients are allowed; allies, ground units, buildings and spell-immune enemies are excluded. Invisible recipients remain eligible for an area aura; an invisible or Hex-suppressed source does not contribute. `ward` permits the native ward category rather than restricting the aura exclusively to ward objects. The absence of an invulnerable permission is interpreted as excluding protected recipients; this interpretation has synthetic coverage, but no Warcraft runtime oracle was executed.
- Native Sphere: `A0E5` (base `Asph`) retains its source chest attachment, native visual missile speed/arc and protected cooldown. Multiple attachment slots rotate and retain separate deadlines. Visual flights retain air/ground origin and destination height even when their source or target is gone, and use the release destination; this deterministic native presentation interpretation is explicitly separate from the weapon's hit delivery. Native Sphere behavior was cross-checked against the [ability insight source](https://github.com/Cokemonkey11/wc3-ability-doc/blob/main/sources/screwthetrees-wc3-editor-ability-insight-document/2023-11-14.md); native hostile-aura immunity/invisibility rules were checked against [Blizzard spell basics](https://classic.battle.net/war3/basics/spellbasics.shtml).

Rendering preserves source mesh opacity through material animation, while native effect attachments retain independent color/opacity. Sphere attachment visibility also suppresses particle emission. `wK` registers `Fu:create849`; callback 10 retains its two independent columns and its separately colored Possession effect. The direct literal-call generator preserves ordered geometry, scale and RGB, and rejects dynamic values/extra control flow. Native models, both weapons, status buff art, ability icons and lifecycle animations use source-owned assets.
- State/content: the promotion advances the runtime slice to r60, bundle schema 38, canonical checksum 55 and snapshot 50. Sphere attachment deadlines, separate visual-ID allocation and visual flights are serializable/canonical state; no presentation time or worker count affects gameplay. Source projections retain their extraction tree and map version.

## Zephyros Memorial / Greater Elemental of Wind

- Entity/source: `h04D` produces `o00E`, completing the first Wind upgrade chain. All ordinary/protected stats, regeneration, organic flying sapper/ward classification, corpse restrictions, economy and production retain the catalog's source identity. The two independent piercing weapons retain their native air splash and ground/structure delivery. Vicious Winds' tooltip does not replace the native weapon's distinct full, medium and outer splash falloff.
- Inventory: building `A0DS` and unit `A0F5` remain Elemental classification markers. `A03Q` is the hidden zero-visibility Channel source-damage dispatcher marker; its source handler's unit/type and child-ability branches do not give this Wind unit an extra source-damage proc. `A0CR` reuses the audited hostile-air zero-linger aura in the same `B026` family. `A0E5` and the additional three-attachment `A0E6` each retain independent Sphere deadlines, native missile art/speed/arc and protected cooldowns. Entry RGBA remains the exact `onUnitEnteredMap` source formula.
- Damage trace: `handleTargetDamageEffects` requires live pre-event health and the `A0C5` marker, then dispatches `o00E` to `vampireCharge(GetEventDamage())`. The reduced event adds mana while ordinary health damage still commits. The below-threshold branch writes/clamps mana. The threshold branch removes the marker, raises native movement speed, grants protected `A0EN` and enables `selfdestructon`; it does not write the triggering mana addition, spend mana or immediately explode. Signed native mana regeneration preserves exact fractional drain, including while disabled or armed. Friendly native mana regeneration and external restoration/combustion act on the same charge pool. Cached controller state survives Hex's projected passive suppression; Hex still prevents native spell orders and suppresses the speed override.
- Native child: `A0EN` is `Asds`, with its own full/partial radii and damage, building factor and air/structure/debris mask. Ground combat units, allies and invulnerable recipients are excluded; physical native ability damage retains armor points without attack-class multipliers or spell resistance. This damage classification and caster death were checked against [Blizzard's Goblin reference](https://classic.battle.net/war3/neutral/goblins.shtml). The death flag describes corpse destruction, not an additional damaging explosion ([native ability insight](https://github.com/Cokemonkey11/wc3-ability-doc/blob/main/sources/screwthetrees-wc3-editor-ability-insight-document/2023-11-14.md)); a fatal charging hit cannot detonate the dead unit. Static terrain destructibles have no native damageable handles in the current engine, so debris destruction remains dormant.
- Order/timing interpretation: the native zero-range autocast approaches a visible eligible enemy to collision contact, independently of the explosion radius, then uses the source unit's native cast point. The explosion remains caster-centered even if recipients move during windup. Stun, order suspension or Hex cancels pending casting; armed state remains and can resume. Contact/acquisition geometry and cast timing are explicit deterministic engine interpretations; no Warcraft runtime oracle was executed.
- Presentation/state: `wK` registers `Gu:create850`, callback 11; the retained projection places both intact columns and the independently colored Tornado effect. Native models, icons, splash weapon, both Sphere inventories and aura status art retain source bindings. Charge mana fraction, marker-removal/armed state and pending cast deadline are serialized/canonical. Runtime slice r61 advances bundle/checksum/snapshot schemas to 39/56/51. The unit-specific dispatcher tuning prevents shared `A0C5` from installing Wind behavior on future Lightning units.
- Verification: synthetic fixtures prove damage without absorption, signed fractional drain, threshold-without-mana-write, one-time arming, collision contact vs explosion radius, native cast-point recovery across workers, caster-centered full/partial falloff, armor-class independence, magic-immune air recipients, ally/ground/invulnerable exclusions, and fatal-hit/no-death-explosion behavior. Reproducible tuning rejects changed immediate orders and extra threshold-branch mana writes. Catalog-wide closure verifies complete inventories, both weapons, upgrades and source costs without independent numeric assertions.
