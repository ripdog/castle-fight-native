# Desert implementation

Retained Castle Fight 9.27/r1 extraction tree: `8ea806dca331ff254995e94e6f0baf225a14bf10`. Builder `X078`, registration `qK` and the resolved `ubpx`/`ubpy` build-card coordinates define implementation order (an absent coordinate on a map-authored `npgr` building is the stock origin). Complete each root and its entire upgrade chain before moving right, then the next row. Only completed entities become buildable. The order is Quillboar Yard → Razormane Abode; Gnoll Tent → Fire Gnoll Hut; Harpy Nest → Nest of Harpy Witch → Nest of Harpy Matriarch; Sandkin Burrow → Monstrous Sandkin Burrow; Ogre Den → Den of the Ogre Lord; Salamander Hatchery; Sacred Peyote; Beacon of the Oasis; Ballista Tower; Temple of Storm; shared Treasure Box.

## Razormane Abode / Razormane

- Identity/source: runtime r154; `n037` (`npgr`) upgrades from `n036` and produces `n02U` (`nrzt`) in the same root's upgrade slot. Protected body life, armor, attack damage/dice, cooldown and range supersede static sentinels. The ordinary Pierce weapon (ground and structure targets, no air), mana pool (maximum, starting, regeneration), acquisition/collision, regeneration, raisable/decaying organic corpse, sapper/ward classification, production, construction/repair/economy and upgrade relationship consume retained versioned catalogs. The second weapon is disabled; the building inventory is empty.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0G7` (`AUts`) is the same intrinsic returned-damage passive as the Quillboar's with its own retained factor. `A0GL` (`AEfk`) is registered object data whose only script consumer is `handleFanOfKnives`, called by the global per-unit damage runtime (`EVENT_UNIT_DAMAGED`, installed for every combat sapper) with the *damaged* unit. The listener requires the Razormane body type, `BlzGetEventDamageType() == DAMAGE_TYPE_NORMAL`, a positive ability level, zero remaining cooldown and current mana at least the native cost; it then issues the immediate `fanofknives` order and `orderCodeAttack` for the same unit with no delay. The importer reconciles those predicates, the retained effective cooldown/mana cost/area/mask and the native damage fields before emitting the profile.
- Trigger vs effect: trigger eligibility is "the Razormane itself took ordinary weapon damage" (authoritative `RetaliationState` of the previous completed tick, shared with ally defense); there is no unit target, approach geometry or range. The effect origin is the caster. Effect eligibility is independent: living hostile air/ground *units* in the retained area that are organic (not mechanical), not invulnerable, not spell immune and not Locust. Structures and wards are not hit. Damage is native spell damage (spell armor-type table and Defend reduction); per-target damage is the retained value unless that times the number of recipients exceeds the retained total cap, in which case the cap is divided evenly (floor). A nonzero retained target limit would keep the nearest recipients; this body's is unlimited.
- Resources/timing: mana is committed at cast and the cooldown starts at the cast; the ordinary cast action (unit cast point/backswing) applies because the unit issues its own order. The script gate and the order are one callback in WC3; the engine evaluates the trigger at the start of the next tick, so the order can lag by the tick granularity. After the cast the unit resumes ordinary combat. A stunned, frozen or dead Razormane cannot cast.
- Known tooltip/object/script discrepancies: the ability is a manual-looking Warden spell that the script also issues from any normal-type damage event; script-authored `UnitDamageTarget` calls that use the same damage type (for example other units' death explosions) are not modeled as triggers, only ordinary weapon impacts that mark retaliation. Native spell damage uses a different WC3 damage type and is correctly excluded.
- State/serialization: the cast reuses existing mana/cooldown/cast-sequence state; the new `FanOfKnives` ability effect and `AttackDamageTrigger` policy are included in canonical hashing, content identity hashing and the snapshot schema (v99).

## Nest of Harpy Witch / Harpy Witch

- Identity/source: runtime r159; `n03B` (`npgr`) upgrades from the Harpy Nest and produces `n032`, the flying Pierce missile body with the retained mana pool; it can itself upgrade to the Matriarch nest. Protected life, armor, attack damage/dice and range supersede static sentinels; targets are ground units and structures only.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A00U` is the shared native Evasion row. `A0KB` (`AIva`, "Life Steal") is a native item-class passive with no script consumer: every ordinary attack hit on a unit heals the attacker by the retained fraction of the damage actually dealt (post armor and defenses), capped by missing health. Its retained mask is `air,ground,enemy` (no organic token), so it applies to mechanical bodies and to melee and ranged weapons alike, matching the tooltip's "damage dealt by Harpy converted to its HP"; structures are not drained. `A0G5` (`AIdf`, "Blind") is an Orb of Darkness: the retained chance (identical for units, heroes and summons) casts the free child `A0GD` (`ANdh`, Drunken Haze) on a landed attack. The child, projected as a native debuff, slows movement and makes attacks miss for the retained durations; its protected 0.1 s cooldown is enforced per attacker (authoritative orb cooldown status) like every orb child, and the hostile-unit mask lists no organic token.
- Scripted spell trace (`A0G9`, `ACpa`, "DST: Harpy auto"): trigger eligibility is the native Parasite's autocast over the retained mask (hostile air or ground, organic, with the sapper body requirement), selected deterministically among candidates in the retained 450 range; approach/cast geometry is the parent's cast range and cast animation. The parent is the native free-body Parasite (retained buff, missile speed and unit-death body). Effect origin: the parent's target. Resources: protected mana cost and cooldown are committed at the cast. Delayed sequence: a uniform draw in the retained 0.1–0.6 second window (strictly after the lower bound, quantized to the first tick not before the draw, keyed by match seed, tick, caster and cast sequence) schedules the callback; when it fires, the amount is the lesser of the script literal and the target's current life, Chaos attack damage of that amount is dealt as a (non-ranged) attack through armor, Hardened Skin, incoming reductions and the damage listeners, and the caster is healed by the *requested amount* (not by the damage that got through) when still alive. A dead or removed target contributes zero. Persistent state: the pending callback is authoritative in the caster's status. A caster that dies during the delay loses its callback (the script would still damage); this is left dormant.
- Known tooltip/object/script discrepancies: tooltip says Blind is 15% for 90% miss and Life Steal 20%; object data says 16%, 65% and 23%, and the importer uses the object data. The extraction summary labels the heal "by actual damage"; the callback heals by the clamped requested amount.
- State/serialization: new status slot, new life-steal passive and miss-chance debuff field (canonical hash, content hash, snapshot schema v102); the Parasite script effect and its child are part of the ability hash.

## Nest of Harpy Matriarch / Harpies' Matriarch

- Identity/source: runtime r160; `n031` (`npgr`, legendary) upgrades from the Witch nest and produces `n034`, the flying legendary body with the retained hero-type splash weapon (ground, air and structures), no mana pool, legendary spell resistance and immunities. Protected life, armor, damage/dice and range supersede static sentinels; all legendary markers (`A070` anti-hex, `A07H` type marker, `A0AH` 70% spell resistance) are the shared native/marker rows.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0H3` (`Asp1`) is a visual-only Sphere attachment; `A0H1` ("DST: Matriarch auto", `ACpa`) is the native free-body Parasite whose mask is hostile air or ground (no organic token), cast range 350, protected cooldown 16 s and zero mana. Its script handler runs when the Parasite takes effect: a recycled dummy owned by the caster's player is created at the *Parasite target's* position, its mana is filled, `A0GM` is granted and the immediate `locustswarm` order issued with a 15-second dummy lifetime, then the Matriarch immediately resumes attacking. `A0GM` (`AUls`) is registered object data: seven locusts released 0.15 s apart, at most two per target, 800 area, 15 s, damage-return factor and threshold, swarm body `u00G` (flying Locust Harpy: spell-type missile weapon `140 + 2d3`, 1.0 s cooldown, targets air/ground/structures, flight speed 425).
- Trigger vs effect: trigger eligibility is the Parasite's (hostile air or ground, sapper body, visible, not invulnerable or spell immune); the swarm's own targets are independent: any hostile unit or structure inside the 800 area of the *fixed dummy position* (not the Matriarch), including spell-immune bodies (the tooltip: pierces spell immunity). The swarm outlives its caster and belongs to the caster's player; its damage is attributed to the Matriarch's identity for retaliation and bounty plumbing.
- Timing/state: the pending swarm is a world-level authoritative action (center, start, expiry and every locust's committed target, strike tick and re-engagement tick; canonical hash and snapshot included). Locust bodies are not simulated: locust *i* is released at `ceil(i * 0.15 s)`; it commits to the nearest eligible target (units before structures; per-target cap counts the other locusts' commitments; ties by identity), flies the straight distance from the swarm center at the retained speed, strikes with the retained spell damage, and — because every landing clears the damage-return threshold — flies back before choosing again (cycle at least one attack cooldown). Structures take the script's 50% reduction (`handler DamageListener…` branch keyed to the swarm body type). The dummy, not the Matriarch, receives the returned health, so the Matriarch is never healed; the return factor therefore has no gameplay effect and is not modeled.
- Known discrepancies / left dormant: the tooltip says the Matriarch "summons a harpy swarm onto an enemy unit"; the script places the swarm at that unit's position as stated above. Locust collision, interception, individual vision and mid-flight retargeting are not simulated (the swarm uses derived timing); a locust never targets a unit that has left the 800 area between commitment and strike only if it is dead.

## Sandkin Burrow / Sandkin

- Identity/source: runtime r161; `n039` (`npgr`) produces `n030` and upgrades to the Monstrous Sandkin Burrow. Protected life, armor and weapon range supersede static sentinels; the Magic splash-class weapon targets ground units and structures (its area fields are unset, so there is no collateral). The unit carries the retained mana pool. Everything else (corpse, collision, classification, production, construction, upgrade) consumes the retained catalogs.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` hidden marker and `A0G8` ("DST: Sandkin abil", `ACpa`), a native free-body Parasite whose retained mask is hostile ground units (range 450), protected cost 35 mana and cooldown 12 s (one ability shared with the Monstrous Sandkin, whose own mana pool overrides the recipe source's). The script (`spellSandCloud`) reads the Parasite *target's* position, chooses the child by the caster's body type, creates a recycled carrier owned by the caster's player at that position for 5.7 seconds, resumes the caster's attack, and registers a one-second periodic callback that makes the carrier cast its area Silence at the stored point five times. Each cast is instantaneous and the child (`A0GA` for Sandkin, `A0HP` for Monstrous Sandkin) is native Silence with zero attacks prevented, no movement or attack-speed change, a miss chance and a 1.2 s buff; both strengths are stored in the one effect and selected by caster body type.
- Trigger vs effect: the Parasite targets ground combat bodies; the cloud affects every hostile air or ground *unit* in its retained area around that fixed point (including spell-immune and structure-attacker bodies is not applicable: spell-immune bodies are skipped by the shared debuff rules). Structure attackers are not affected because the engine's miss-chance modifier exists only for unit attackers; that remains dormant. Clouds from different casts overlap by refreshing the same buff; the highest miss chance applies.
- Timing/state: the carrier is authoritative world state (center, expiry, next pulse, pulses left); the first pulse is one period after the cast, the fifth at 5.0 s, the carrier lapses at 5.7 s; the 1.2 s buff lasts past each pulse.
- Known discrepancies: the tooltip says "cooldown 12 s" for the Sandkin and "10 s" for the Monstrous one; the shared protected overlay gives both 12 s, and the importer uses the object data. "Dim enemy vision" is presentation; the retained effect is the miss chance only.

## Beacon of the Oasis, Sacred Peyote and Temple of Storm

These three utility buildings share one engine framework: the native building trigger (an inert Parasite whose retained mask is hostile ground, structure, mechanical and invulnerable bodies, range 99999, protected mana cost and 1-second cooldown plus the building's retained mana pool, regeneration starting empty) decides *when* the building fires; a script child chooses its own recipients. Mechanical, invulnerable and structure triggers are all admitted (the same mask semantics as the other trigger buildings). Runtime r169.

- **Beacon of the Oasis** (`h079`, `A0GE`, panel slot `(3,1)`): the script draws a random ally from the first non-empty tier of three ordered group filters: living allied combat bodies missing more than the retained 200 life, then any wounded ally, then an ally whose mana percentage is above zero and below 99.9%; then adds the retained 175 life and 40 mana (clamped to maximums; mana only for units with a pool) and plays an effect. Draws are deterministic ranks keyed by match seed, building, ability and cast sequence. The marker `A07E` is the shared hidden Channel row. The tooltip's "cooldown 5 s" is the mana cadence (protected cost over regeneration), not the 1-second ability cooldown.
- **Sacred Peyote** (`h07A`, `A0GF`, slot `(2,1)`): picks a random alive hostile combat body that is vulnerable and does not carry the tentacle marker; if a shield absorbs the cast (consuming it, as for the other negative building spells) nothing happens; otherwise the owner's dummy casts the item illusion (`AM0y`, `AIil`) on it. The result is an illusion of that body fighting for the building's player with the retained duration (60 s), damage dealt (125%) and damage received (75%) multipliers, the target's current life and its weapon; abilities, corpse and the on-hit proc passives named for mirror images are stripped, matching the existing illusion machinery. The illusion appears at the target's position and relies on the movement collision repair.
- **Temple of Storm** (`n03A`, legendary, `A0GK`, slot `(1,2)`): the script enables the weather art and creates an owner-player carrier with two abilities for 6 s: `A0G4` (`ACsi`) cast at a map-central point with a map-wide area, and `A0GC` (`ANpi`) as a map-wide immolation. Modeled as a one-time map-wide hostile debuff (retained miss chance, movement and attack-speed penalties, 8 s) plus an authoritative pulsing damage action for the carrier's life (20 damage per retained one-second interval to every hostile air or ground unit; pulses fall strictly before the carrier's expiry, i.e. five pulses). Structures are not damaged, and structure attackers are unaffected by the miss chance (the engine models miss chance only on unit attackers).
- Left dormant: weather visuals; the Peyote's temporary vision reveal for unseen targets; carrier positions (the Sandstorm is global).
- State: the Sandstorm pulse action is authoritative world state (canonical hash, snapshot schema v104); illusions use the existing illusion status.
