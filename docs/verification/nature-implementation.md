# Nature implementation

Retained Castle Fight 9.27/r1 extraction tree: `8ea806dca331ff254995e94e6f0baf225a14bf10`. Builder `X01A` ("Nature Builder", `uabi` `A06E,Aeth,AInv,A005,A001,Ahrp,Avul`), registration `IK` and the resolved `ubpx`/`ubpy` build-card coordinates define implementation order. Complete each root and its entire upgrade chain before moving right, then the next row. Only completed entities become buildable. The order is Spider Egg Clutch → Spider Egg Nest → Spider Egg Hatchery; Furbolg Hut → Furbolg Abode, then its second branch Enigma; Ancient of Growth → Ancient of Life → Ancient of Eternity; Ancient of Slumber → Ancient of Hibernation; Emerald Dragon Roost; Monolith; Ancient Guardian; Ancient of Gale; Ancient Protector; Ancient of Wonders; the shared Treasure Box (already promoted). (`Aeth`, `AInv`, `Ahrp`, `Avul` and the build-menu abilities are the shared builder body already used by every promoted race.)

## Spider Egg Clutch / Black Spider

- Identity/source: runtime r225; `h002` (`hbla`) produces `n00U` (`nspb`) in the top-left slot and upgrades to the Spider Egg Nest. Protected life, armor, movement and the Pierce weapon (ground and structures, no air), acquisition/collision, regeneration, raisable/decaying organic corpse (`can-raise-decays`), sapper/ward classification, production, construction/repair/economy and the upgrade edge come from the catalogs. The building has no ability or inventory.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0AO` (`Aven`, "Envenomed Spears") and `A09H` (`ANcl`, "TypeMurloc"). `A0AO` is registered object data with no script consumer or hidden proxy: the shared native poison primitive (`warcraft-poison-v1`, also used by the Wyvern Rider's `A038`) with its damage per second, attack-speed factor, movement factor, duration, hero duration and stacking type all projected from the retained row; organic air/ground recipients only, per-source stacking, non-lethal pulses. `A09H` is the same companion marker the Murlocs use (`EventListener_add_CompanionSpawning` adds an unconditional same-type copy, `spawnSyncedCompanions` handles training and Heroic Shrine rolls); one ability object, one binding.
- Known discrepancies: the authored tooltip says the poison reduces attack *and movement* speed by 10%; the retained row has a zero movement factor and a 0.1 attack-speed factor, so the engine follows the row (attack speed only).
- Nature becomes selectable with this completed root; later roots remain unavailable until their own mechanics are verified.

## Spider Egg Nest / Giant Spider

Protected life, armor, movement and the Pierce weapon (ground and structures, no air), the other catalog-owned fields as for the Black Spider, and a raisable/decaying organic corpse come from the catalogs. All four are registered object data with no script consumer except the protected-field overlay for `A0AR` (cooldown 18 s, source function `xD`); no hidden proxy exists. `A0AP` is the shared native poison primitive. `A0AQ` is the same native passive death-spawn as the Hydra's (`Aspy`): the retained count of the retained child `n00T` ("Forest Spider", a plain promoted melee body whose only ability is the companion marker) at the dying body's position. `A0AR` is the unit's `udaa` default-active autocast.
- Spell trace (`A0AR`): trigger eligibility — an enemy air or ground unit (the map widened the stock air-only mask to ground and air) within the cast range (450) that is visible, alive, not invulnerable or Locust, not already bound, and in combat (attacking a unit that is in combat, or attacking a building within its own attack range), nearest first; spell immunity does not matter because Web is physical (as for the existing net). Approach/cast geometry — the order is a ranged targeted cast; the unit stops within range (the retained `Rng1`). Effect origin — a homing missile from the caster (retained speed 1500) that binds on impact. Effect eligibility — any hostile body alive at impact that is not invulnerable. Resources — no mana, and the protected 18-second cooldown; the retained duration (10 s) is shared by heroes. Persistence — an air body is lowered to the ground for the duration (retained lowering time, height and melee range) and every body is rooted but may still attack.
- Engine change: the ensnare profile gained a target mask (the scripted net keeps flying-only), and native autocast target selection accepts the Ensnare effect. Web is the same authoritative `Ensnare` status as the net. Snapshot schema 120, checksum schema 117.
- Known discrepancies: the tooltip says the poison reduces attack *and movement* speed by 15%; the retained row has a zero movement factor, so the engine follows the row.

## Spider Egg Hatchery / Brood Mother

- Identity/source: runtime r229; `h00R` (`hbla`) produces `n01W` (`nsbm`), upgrading the Spider Egg Nest (the end of the line). Protected life, armor, movement, the mana pool (100, +2/s) and the Pierce weapon (ground and structures, no air) come from the catalogs; raisable/decaying organic corpse.
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0AS` (`Aweb`, "Web"), `A0AU` (`Aspy`, "Spawn Giant Spiders"), `A0AT` (`Aven`, "Envenomed Spears"), `A0AV` (`ACpa`, "Infest") and `A09H` (the shared companion marker). `A0AT` is the shared native poison (one source per recipient stacks damage; non-lethal). `A0AU` is the native passive death-spawn of the retained count of the Giant Spider (`n01N`). `A0AS` is the Giant Spider's Web with the Brood Mother's own retained duration (12 s) and the protected 18-second cooldown. `A0AV` is a *stock, unmodified* damaging Parasite (only duration, damage, cost, range and the summon were edited) and the sole ability the unit lists as default-active (`udaa`).
- Scripted spell trace (`A0AV` via `RaceNatureAbilities`, semantic kind `registered-infest-then-enable-autocasts`): trigger eligibility — a hostile visible organic non-hero air or ground combat body in the retained range (500) that is not invulnerable or spell-immune. Approach/cast geometry — a targeted ranged cast. Effect origin — a homing Parasite missile from the caster (retained speed 1900); on arrival the Parasite buff (`B022`) lasts the retained 10 s for every body and its damage is a lethal poison (retained 20/s, one clock per host; a second Infest replaces the first). If the host dies while the buff stands, the retained count (2) of the retained body (Forest Spider `n00T`, permanent, summoned) appear at its corpse for the caster's owner. Resources — the protected 50 mana and 10 s cooldown. Delayed sequence — `UnitSpellClosure…RaceNatureAbilities5` waits 3 s after every cast, issues `webon` (Web autocast on) and waits 3 s more, then issues `parasiteon` (Infest autocast on again) and an attack order. Because only `A0AV` is default-active, Web starts **disabled**: the Brood Mother does not web anything until three seconds after her first Infest cast. The attack order is only the lane re-issue the engine already performs. The chain dies with the caster.
- Engine change: the Parasite action can carry a damage poison (`NativeParasiteState.damage`) applied at impact; a Parasite's host death spawns a promoted combat body through the same summon path as `Aspy` children (previously only non-combat bodies were created); `ParasiteChild::Infest` with a two-stage pending script (`PendingParasiteScript.stage`); automatic abilities another ability's script owns now start with autocast off. Snapshot schema 121, checksum schema 118.
- Known discrepancies: the tooltip says Infest deals "100 initial damage plus 20 dmg/sec"; the retained row has no initial damage, so the engine follows the row. The tooltip also says the poison reduces *movement* speed by 25%; the row's factor is zero.

## Furbolg Hut / Furbolg Shaman

- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` (the shared hidden Channel marker) and `A0C0` ("rejuvenation (dummy)", `Ahea`, order `heal`). `A0C0` is a native Heal whose own healing is 0.01 hit points (an authored marker value) and whose retained row names a 9999 mana cost that the protected-field overlay replaces with 50 mana and a 16-second cooldown; the script closure registered for its order (`UnitSpellClosure…RaceNatureAbilities`) is the real effect: a free dummy at the caster, owned by the caster's player, casts native Rejuvenation `A0AW` (`Arej`, `Brej`) on the same target (the dummy has unlimited mana, the lifetime is 2 s), and 0.8 s later the closure re-issues the unit's attack order. There is no hidden proxy beyond that dummy.
- Spell trace: trigger eligibility — the native Heal order: a wounded friendly non-mechanical non-ancient ground unit (invulnerable included) within the retained range (250) of the caster. Approach/cast geometry — a targeted cast at the trigger range. Effect origin — the target itself (the dummy is created at the caster, whose position never matters because the retained range of the effect, 800, exceeds the trigger range). Effect eligibility — independent of the trigger: friendly organic bodies, vulnerable or invulnerable (the stock mask without the ancient exclusion; irrelevant because ancients never trigger it). Resources — the protected 50 mana and 16 s cooldown; the dummy costs nothing. Delayed sequence — Rejuvenation heals the retained 200 hit points evenly over its retained 10 seconds as regeneration (heroes included); a recast replaces it. The resume order 0.8 s later is the lane re-issue the engine already performs.
- Engine change: the native Heal profile gained an optional Rejuvenation (a regeneration buff carrying the retained native buff identity, so it participates in the shared dispel/steal family). Snapshot schema 122, checksum schema 119.
- Known discrepancies: the 0.01 hit point trigger heal is below the engine's whole-point health resolution and is recorded rather than applied. Warcraft applies Rejuvenation continuously; the engine applies the regeneration through its shared per-tick regeneration accumulation.

## Enigma / Visioneer

- Identity/source: runtime r233; `h071` (`hbla`) is the Furbolg Hut's second upgrade and produces `e00G` (`edry`), a unit with two ordinary enabled weapons. Protected life, armor, movement and the mana pool (100, +2/s) come from the catalogs; raisable/decaying organic corpse. The building has no ability.
- Weapons: the first Chaos weapon (range 1000) lists only `enemies`, `structure`, `ward`, `item` and `debris`, with no movement-class token, so it is the structure weapon of the secondary-weapon primitive; the second Chaos weapon (range 550, `air,ground,enemies`) is the unit weapon. The supplement's secondary missile speed for `e00G` is its flight speed. (`ward` is the map-wide combat classification and does not open a unit mask.)
- Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` (shared marker), `A0FV` ("Demolisher", `ANde`) and `Adtg` ("True Sight"), all registered object data with no script consumer or hidden proxy. `A0FV` is the object-native passive Demolish (enemy structures only, retained chance 100%, ×3; heroes and units are multiplied by zero — the row's unit/hero multipliers are inert because the mask lists structures only). `Adtg` is native True Sight: an always-on detection aura (detection type 3) whose retained range (1200) is its only radius, since the row's area is `-`.
- Effect trace (`Adtg`): origin — the Visioneer itself; eligibility — invisible bodies of either team inside the radius become visible to the bearer's team (the engine has Wind Walk's and the class marker's invisibility only; the row's `vuln,invu` tokens mean both vulnerable and invulnerable bodies are revealed). Resources — none. Persistence — recomputed every tick while the bearer is alive.
- Engine change: `PassiveUnitEffect::TrueSight`, applied when unit snapshots are built (team detection mask). Snapshot schema 124, checksum schema 121.
- Known discrepancies: no Warcraft runtime oracle is available for the exact detection radius (range versus a stock area default); the row's range is used.

## Ancient of Growth / Dryad

- Identity/source: runtime r236; `h013` (`hbla`) produces `e006` (`edry`) in the third slot of the top row and upgrades to the Ancient of Life. - Complete inventory/control flow: `A0CV` and `A03Q` (shared markers; `A03Q` gates the source-damage listener `handleSourceDamageEffects` for this body), `A004` ("natures touch", `Ahea`) and `A0AY` ("spell resist", `AIsr`). `A0AY` is the shared native spell resistance (retained 15% damage reduction from spells). `A004` is the native Heal trigger with the retained 75 hit points, a 250 range, and the protected 35 mana and 15 s cooldown.
- Scripted spell trace (`A004`, closure `RaceNatureAbilities2`): trigger eligibility — the native Heal order: a wounded friendly non-mechanical non-ancient ground unit (invulnerable included) within range. Effect — the target gains the retained 75 hit points and, in the same call, the closure installs the stock item ability `AId2` ("Item Armor Bonus (+2)") on it with `addProtectedAbility`; adding an ability the unit already owns changes nothing, so the **+2 armor is granted once per unit and never stacks** (it is permanent and not a dispellable buff). A flash effect plays on the target. Delayed sequence — 0.6 s after the cast the closure re-issues the attack order (the lane re-issue the engine already performs). Resources — protected 35 mana and 15 s cooldown.
- Source-damage listener trace (`dryadDispelProc` in `handleSourceDamageEffects`): trigger eligibility — each landed weapon hit on a unit whose attacker is a Dryad carrying `A03Q`; one integer roll in 0..99 against the retained 15. Effect — `UnitRemoveBuffsEx(target, positive, no negative, magical, ...)`: the attacked unit alone (no area) loses its positive magical buffs. The roll and the removal ignore spell immunity. Resources — none.
- Engine change: the native Heal profile gained a Nature's Touch descriptor (a permanent non-stacking armor grant and, for the Keeper, a Spirit Wolf order attempt). `PassiveUnitEffect::AttackDispel` resolves at the attack roll into a triggered dispel; the positive-buff dispel (a native buff states its own sign; a script modifier is positive when all of its effects help the bearer) is separate from the Siren's full dispel. Per-body listener branches are retained as `unit_source_damage_rules` because the shared dispatcher marker `A03Q` belongs to several bodies. Snapshot schema 125, checksum schema 122.
- Known discrepancies: the 15% roll is taken from the engine's per-attack proc stream rather than the map's `GetRandomInt`; positivity of a buff without a native identity is inferred from its effects.
